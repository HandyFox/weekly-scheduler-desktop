Weekly Assignment Scheduler
===========================

Plan each week in a grid and print a sheet for every student plus a teacher copy.
It opens in your web browser but runs entirely on this computer. No internet needed.

Starting it
  Open "Weekly Assignment Scheduler" from the Start menu or the desktop.
  Your browser opens the scheduler. A small calendar icon appears by the clock
  (look under the ^ arrow if you don't see it).
  Click the icon to open the scheduler again; right-click it for the folder with
  your schedule, or Quit. The Quit button in the top corner of the page works too.

Where your schedule is kept
  Documents\Weekly Assignment Scheduler\scheduler.db
  A backup copy goes into the "backups" folder next to it every time the
  scheduler starts (the last 15 are kept). Settings -> Your data can show the
  backups and go back to one.

Already using the scheduler?
  Settings -> Your data -> "Use a different scheduler.db..." and pick your file.
  Your current schedule is backed up first.

Updating
  Run the newer installer. Your schedule is not touched.

Uninstalling
  Settings -> Apps -> Weekly Assignment Scheduler -> Uninstall.
  Your schedule stays in Documents.

Using it from a tablet on the same Wi-Fi
  Make a shortcut to WeeklyScheduler.exe with --share after it, e.g.
    "%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Weekly Assignment Scheduler\WeeklyScheduler.exe" --share
  Anyone on that network can then open it, so only do this on a network you trust.

Windows warned "Windows protected your PC"?
  The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click "More info" and
  then "Run anyway".
